# Illustrations

Thirteen scenes from **[Storyset](https://storyset.com/)** (Freepik Company).

## Licence — attribution is required

Storyset's free licence permits commercial use **on the condition that Storyset is
credited with a visible, followable link**. That credit is not optional and not a
formality: it is the term under which these files may be used at all. It is
rendered on the landing page footer, not buried here.

    Illustrations by <a href="https://storyset.com/">Storyset</a>

If a scene is ever used on a surface that cannot carry the credit, the correct move
is to drop the scene, not the credit.

## Recolouring

Each file has been recoloured from Storyset's own accent family onto the Aegis blue
ramp, so the illustrations belong to the product rather than sitting beside it:

    #547cef -> #1570ef   --blue-600   (Storyset primary)
    #6880c8 -> #175cd3   --blue-700
    #4262c7 -> #0b3b8f   --blue-900
    #7d9bf5 -> #60a5fa   --blue-400
    #92a9f7 -> #bfdbfe   --blue-200

Only that accent family was touched. The greys, the line work and the figures' own
tones are untouched — those are the drawing, not the brand, and repainting them
would flatten scenes that were composed with them.
