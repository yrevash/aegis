# Skill: Closing and resolving customer requests

Use when the customer asks for their request to be closed, resolved, or marked done.

- Confirm the specific request (id, current status, opened date) before acting; never guess.
- State the closure policy plainly: a request is closed once the customer confirms the
  outcome, or after 30 days with no reply on a waiting_customer request.
- Closing a request is a **write action** — it must go through the approval gate; propose
  it, do not assert it as done.
- If the customer is enterprise-tier, note their account manager should be CC'd on the
  resolution.
- Close by confirming the expected timeline (an approver reviews a proposed closure within
  one business day).
