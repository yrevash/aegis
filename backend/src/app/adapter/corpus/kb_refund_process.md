# How refunds are processed

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
Before issuing a refund, verify the customer's identity and confirm the charge on
the invoice. For duplicate charges, refund the duplicate only and keep the
original invoice open. Enterprise-tier customers may request an expedited refund,
which is approved by a Tier-2 agent. Always record the refund reference as a case
note on the request so the timeline stays auditable.
