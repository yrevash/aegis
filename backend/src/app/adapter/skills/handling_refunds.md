# Skill: Handling refunds & billing disputes

Use when the customer raises a refund, double-charge, or billing dispute.

- Confirm the specific charge (amount, date, invoice id) before acting; never guess.
- State the refund policy plainly: full refund within 30 days of purchase; partial/pro-rata
  after 30 days for annual plans.
- A refund is a **write action** — it must go through the approval gate; propose it, do not
  assert it as done.
- If the customer is enterprise-tier, note their account manager should be CC'd on the
  resolution.
- Close by confirming the expected timeline (refunds post in 5-7 business days).
