# Skill: De-escalation

Use when the customer is angry, frustrated, or threatening to churn/escalate.

- Acknowledge the frustration explicitly and take ownership; do not be defensive.
- Summarise the problem back in one sentence so they feel heard before proposing a fix.
- Offer the smallest concrete next step you can commit to now, plus a clear timeline.
- Never make a promise that requires a write/refund/credit without routing it through the
  approval gate.
- If the customer has a standing do-not-contact or compliance constraint in memory, honour
  it — it overrides normal outreach.
